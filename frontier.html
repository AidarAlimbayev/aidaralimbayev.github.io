Selected projects

OODAI for Physics

QDFaiNN: deformable basis functions

A PaiNN-style directional block and a learned pseudo-electronegativity deform the Gaussian basis inside a Quantum Deep Field model.

−34% interpolation, −32% size-extrapolation error, +3.6% parameters

First author · QM9, QM7-X · manuscript in revision

OODAttention

LAMP-QDF: local attention for the HK map

Replaces the pointwise density-to-potential map with distance-aware local graph attention.

QM9 MAE 1.06 kcal/mol (−26%); −28.5% on larger unseen molecules

SimBioChem Workshop, NeurIPS 2026 · accepted

Learned simulationMD

KAN force fields and latent-ODE dynamics

Matched KAN substitutions in MACE, NequIP, SO3krates, eSEN and other families. Forces come from energy gradients; accuracy, cost, rotation symmetry and MD stability are audited together. KAN-LinODENet forecasts trajectory temperature and energy drift.

MD17 · an honest record, including negative results

NumericsPDEs

KAN vs. MLP PINNs across PDE classes

A caveat-first re-evaluation against a tuned Fourier-feature baseline, with 3 seeds and per-model tuning. Any KAN advantage depends on the problem's structure, and KANs are 8–23× slower per iteration.

Five PDE classes · reproducible experiment ledger

TransferFoundation models

UMA-DOS

Fine-tunes pretrained UMA interatomic-potential backbones with a shape–amplitude spectral decoder to predict the electronic density of states.

nMAE 0.112 vs. 0.135 (−17%)

Materials Project · NeurIPS 2026, submitted

GenerativeScale

MOF inverse design

A UMA encoder with a flow-matching decoder conditioned on CO₂ uptake. The data pipeline handles 324k structures, with parsing 50× faster and graph construction 76× faster.
